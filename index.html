About

Computer engineer based in Banda Aceh. I build small tools for research, daily needs, and the occasional fun experiment.

Curious about space and the stars, I also keep an eye on global dynamics—especially economics—and how large systems evolve over time. I'm fascinated by how the universe moves and changes from one moment to the next.

This space is a collection of random explorations, so don't expect anything too spectacular—just some rural engineering from a quiet corner of the universe.

"Syah" (meaning "king") originates from Persia and appears in KBBI with the same meaning. It has been used by the rulers of the Perlak Kingdom since around 840 AD. "Raja" is the Indonesian term derived from Indian languages, while "king" is the English equivalent. Combined, "KingSyah" can be interpreted as "King of Kings."
